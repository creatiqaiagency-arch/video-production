## Expressions library (`face` key)

`neutral`, `happy`, `smug`, `unimpressed`, `shock`, `worried`, `sad`, `determined`, `panic`, `shout`, `focused`, `blink`, `bewildered` (the "…Oh my God" look: half-lidded eyes, flat mouth, used in shot 06 and the final shot as a deliberate callback).

## Continuity rules

- Eli's hoodie is red in **every** shot, including the flashback (where it's sepia-toned but still the warmest value).
- Noah's cap is always on. When Noah is "missing" from the workshop, his **yellow cap is NOT there**. Only his empty stool and his blue tool tray remain.
- **The backup sensor** is a small blue box on the robot's **left** side with a tiny blue LED. It must be visible in the workshop shots (planted when Noah says "I added a backup on the left") and again in the final.
- **The main sensor** is an orange box on the robot's front with an orange LED that flickers when it fails.
- Robot design: low 4-wheel chassis, dark grey body with a warm rim, a red stripe (Eli's colour) and a blue stripe (Noah's colour). The two colours on the robot symbolise that it was built together.
- The photo on the workshop desk shows Eli (red) and Noah (yellow cap) with an early prototype.
