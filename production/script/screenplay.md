# WHAT IF YOU COULD HEAR EVERYONE'S THOUGHTS?

Original IFVERSE-inspired animated film · target 5–6 min · 16:9 (letterboxed) · client script.
Genre: superpower fantasy → psychological drama → emotional reversal.
Emotion arc: curiosity → excitement → anxiety → vulnerability → relief.
Core question: would knowing everyone's thoughts make your life better — or destroy your relationships?
Dialogue lines with audio tags live in `vo_lines.json` (ids in brackets below).

## ACT 1 — The power everyone wishes they had

### S1 The first thought (café)
VO [s01_vo1] What if you could hear what EVERYONE was thinking? Every secret. Every lie. Even what your crush actually thinks about you.
VO [s01_vo2] Well, one morning, Noah woke up with exactly that power.
BARISTA (spoken) [s01_barista_say] Here you go. Have a great day!
BARISTA (thought) [s01_barista_th1] Please don't spill it. Please don't spill it.
*The coffee slips from her hand.*  NOAH [s01_noah1] WHAT THE—   BARISTA (thought) [s01_barista_th2] I'm getting fired.
Direction: open on barista's smile while a second voice emerges behind her head; glowing thought bubble
appears a fraction before the sound finishes. ECU Noah's eyes — pupils reflect barista + falling cup.
Low-angle slow-mo of the cup rotating; time freezes, resumes, cup hits floor.
Sound: café → isolated inner voice → ceramic impact → silence.

### S2 Testing whether it's real (street)
VO [s02_vo1] At first, he thought he was losing his mind. But when he walked outside, he realized he could hear every single person around him.
BUSINESSMAN (spoken) [s02_biz_say] Yes, boss. Almost at the office. (thought) [s02_biz_th] I'm still twenty minutes away.
WOMAN (thought) [s02_woman_th] Did I leave the oven on?   MAN (thought) [s02_man_th] Why is that kid staring at me?
NOAH [s02_noah1] Oh my god. This is actually real.
Direction: side-tracking shot following Noah; thoughts as holographic windows with miniature scenes
(boss beside a clock; glowing oven). Third thought: punch in on the stranger staring back. Soft pop per window.

### S3 The first real advantage (classroom)
VO [s03_vo1] And once Noah figured out how it worked, he realized something even crazier. He could use it to get basically anything he wanted.
TEACHER [s03_teacher1] Noah. What's the answer to question seven?  NOAH [s03_noah1] Uh...
TEACHER (thought) [s03_teacher_th] Please don't say C again. It's B.  NOAH [s03_noah2] B!  TEACHER [s03_teacher2] ...Correct.
VO [s03_vo2] Suddenly, school wasn't so difficult anymore.
Direction: wide classroom; room desaturates except a luminous "B" above the teacher; camera follows the B
to Noah's desk. Smash-cut teacher shocked / Noah smug. 3-shot montage of answers. Pens, rising comedic rhythm, bell.

### S4 What his crush thinks (hallway)
VO [s04_vo1] But there was one person Noah wanted to read more than anyone. Maya. The girl he'd been too scared to talk to for almost a year.
MAYA (thought) [s04_maya_th1] Wait... Noah actually looks kinda cute today.  NOAH (whisper) [s04_noah1] No way.
MAYA (thought) [s04_maya_th2] Why is he smiling like that?  NOAH [s04_noah2] Oh.
Direction: warm hallway; Maya far end, Noah small in FG; long-lens push compresses distance. Pink handwritten
thought. Romantic swell → second thought in a different colour → hard cut to Noah's awkward grin; record scratch.

### S5 The problem nobody expected (cafeteria doors)
VO [s05_vo1] For the first few hours, Noah thought he'd become the luckiest person alive. Until he walked into the school cafeteria.
NOAH [s05_noah1] Wait. Wait, hold on—
Direction: Noah enters through double doors, camera locked behind him; one thought shape per student; bubbles
multiply; room seems to expand; push into the overlapping shapes until they fill the screen.

## ACT 2 — Knowing too much

### S6 Information overload
VO [s06_vo1] You see, hearing one person's thoughts was amazing. Hearing TWO HUNDRED people's thoughts at once? Not so much.
THOUGHTS [s06_c1..c8] I'm starving. / Why did she leave me on read? / I hate Mondays. / Did I fail chemistry? / Does anyone even like me? …
VO [s06_vo2] Everyone was thinking something different. And Noah couldn't turn any of it off.
Direction: camera orbits Noah; cafeteria stretches into infinite space; layered translucent panels; crowd multiplies;
room sound replaced by thought voices; peak = rapid cuts eyes / hands over ears / overhead of countless windows. Hard-cut sound.

### S7 He can't escape the voices
VO [s07_vo1] He tried covering his ears. Nothing. He tried headphones. Still nothing. He even locked himself in an empty bathroom.
NOAH [s07_noah1] ARE YOU KIDDING ME?
Direction: ECU hands over ears; profile with huge headphones, thought lines pass through; wide symmetrical bathroom,
glowing shapes pass through the walls. Physical sound muffled, thoughts crisp.

### S8 The first thought that actually hurts (hallway)
VO [s08_vo1] But the worst thing Noah heard that day wasn't from a stranger. It came from Leo. His best friend since they were kids.
LEO [s08_leo_say1] Yo, you coming over Friday?  LEO (thought) [s08_leo_th1] I can't keep doing this.
NOAH [s08_noah1] ...Doing what?  LEO [s08_leo_say2] What?  LEO (thought) [s08_leo_th2] I have to tell him. He's gonna hate me.
Direction: normal hallway, chaos removed. Leo taps Noah's shoulder; stable two-shot; on the thought, CU Noah, background
darkens, words appear phrase by phrase; cut back to Leo who has no idea. One deep tone; silence after "hate me".

### S9 Noah starts imagining the worst
VO [s09_vo1] Noah couldn't stop thinking about what he'd heard. Was Leo planning to ditch him? Had he been lying this whole time? And the more Noah listened, the worse it got.
LEO (thought) [s09_leo_th1] I wish he'd stop asking.  [s09_leo_th2] Friday. I have to do it Friday.
VO [s09_vo2] By the end of the day, Noah was convinced his best friend was hiding something terrible.
Direction: Leo closes a laptop as Noah approaches; Leo ends a phone call abruptly; Leo walks away while Noah's imagined
scenarios appear in dark grainy monochrome panels (speculation ≠ verified thought).

### S10 Maya's thoughts make things worse
MAYA [s10_maya_say1] Hey, Noah. You okay?  (thought) [s10_maya_th1] He seems really upset.  NOAH [s10_noah1] I'm fine.
MAYA (thought) [s10_maya_th2] I want to help, but I don't know what to say.  NOAH [s10_noah2] Then don't say anything!
MAYA [s10_maya_say2] ...What?
VO [s10_vo1] And that's when Noah realized something. Just because he could hear a thought didn't mean he understood it.
Direction: close two-shot, Maya reaches for his arm; warm thought colour vs harsh purple noise around Noah; when he snaps,
remove every overlay; hold Maya's hurt reaction a full beat; no camera shake.

### S11 The growing suspicion
VO [s11_vo1] But there was still one question he couldn't get out of his head. What was Leo planning to tell him on Friday?
LEO (thought) [s11_leo_th] Tomorrow's going to change everything.
Direction: classroom clock → phone showing Thursday evening; Noah watches Leo leave school; date graphic Thursday → Friday; low clock tick.

## ACT 3 — The one thought he wishes he'd never heard

### S12 Friday arrives (courtyard)
VO [s12_vo1] Friday finally came. And Noah wasn't going to wait around for Leo to tell him.
NOAH [s12_noah1] So. What were you gonna tell me?  LEO [s12_leo1] What?  NOAH [s12_noah2] Don't act like you don't know.
LEO (thought) [s12_leo_th] Oh, no. Not like this.
Direction: extreme wide, empty courtyard, Noah under a tree, Leo approaches; hold longer; camera moves closer until they
occupy opposite sides; subdued violet thought behind Leo. Wind, distant sports practice, low tension bed.

### S13 He exposes what he knows
NOAH [s13_noah1] You've been hiding something from me all week.  LEO [s13_leo1] Noah, just let me explain.
LEO (thought) [s13_leo_th] He's gonna think I'm abandoning him.  NOAH [s13_noah2] So you ARE abandoning me?
LEO [s13_leo2] How did you—  NOAH [s13_noah3] I know what you're thinking, Leo!  [Silence.]
Direction: slow track around both; on "abandoning him" sharp CU of Noah's eyes with words projected across his face;
ECU Leo eyes widen, no SFX; pull back to wide with noticeable distance.

### S14 The real reason
LEO [s14_leo1] I got accepted into a school overseas.  NOAH [s14_noah1] ...What?
LEO [s14_leo2] Full scholarship. My mom's already sorting everything out.  NOAH [s14_noah2] And you were just gonna leave without telling me?
LEO [s14_leo3] No! I was trying to figure out how to tell you.  LEO (thought) [s14_leo_th] Please just be happy for me.
Direction: Leo pulls a folded acceptance letter; insert of letter (fictional school, scholarship, departure date);
violet overlays fade; wide with the letter between them; minimal score.

### S15 The emotional low point
NOAH [s15_noah1] You said you couldn't keep doing this.  LEO [s15_leo1] Yeah. Pretending everything was normal while I knew I was leaving.
NOAH [s15_noah2] And you thought I'd hate you?  LEO [s15_leo2] I was scared you'd think I chose a new life over being your friend.
[Pause.] LEO [s15_leo3] I wanted you to be the first person I told.
Direction: restrained eye-level close-ups; believable hesitation/sadness; Noah looks down at the letter; brief memory of
both as kids playing; music enters after the last line.

### S16 The realization
VO [s16_vo1] And suddenly, Noah understood. All day, he'd been hearing people's thoughts. But he'd been treating every passing fear, every unfinished sentence, and every private worry like it was the whole truth. He could hear what people were thinking. He still didn't know what they meant.
Direction: Noah's face; revisit Maya / teacher / Leo moments, now showing the full conversations alongside the thoughts;
end on the two friends in the courtyard, no text on screen.

### S17 Choosing what to listen to
NOAH [s17_noah1] Dude. You're actually getting a full scholarship?  LEO [s17_leo1] Yeah.
NOAH [s17_noah2] That's insane. Why didn't you just tell me?  LEO [s17_leo2] I was trying to.
NOAH [s17_noah3] ...I'm happy for you, man.  LEO (thought) [s17_leo_th] Thank god.  [They laugh.]
Direction: faint thought windows still appear in the background but Noah stops looking at them; camera favours Leo's
expression; small awkward hug; slow pull-out to the wide courtyard composition.

### Closing sting — The romance question
VO [s18_vo1] Of course, Noah still had one person he was too scared to ask about.
MAYA [s18_maya1] Hey. You want to walk home together?  (thought, small) [s18_maya_th] Please say yes.
NOAH [s18_noah1] Yeah. I'd like that.
Direction: school entrance, warm late-afternoon light; Maya enters left; small understated thought; hold Noah's
genuine smile; they walk into the street together.
