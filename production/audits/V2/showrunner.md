# Showrunner audit — V2
SCORE: 6/10
VERDICT: V2 is a real step up from V1, and the improvement shows where it matters most. The school now has depth: the 3/4 hallway (s04a, s05a, s08c), the one-point corridor (s04b, s08e, s10f), the classroom reverse (s03d, s03f) and the perspective cafeteria (s05c) all read as places rather than stage flats. Act 3 finally has a second axis, with Leo against the tree and skyline, and the low gold sun with rays in s15a–s17e makes the reconciliation feel warm. Key thoughts are mostly readable now (s02e, s04e, s04g, s08d, s08f, s09e, s13b). The SAID/THOUGHT replays happen in the original rooms instead of a UI void, and Leo is properly planted in Act 1. But I still would not publish this on a channel with millions of subscribers. The first 30 seconds, which decide retention, still contain broken or placeholder shots: Noah lies above his bed and a leg pokes through it (s01d), the crash is an abstract brown void (s01j), and all three teaser thoughts are unreadable, including the "crush" hook (s01a–s01c). About half the film is still flat façades parallel to the lens: the café, the street, the school exterior, the courtyard building and the sunset ending. Noah's half of the Act 3 coverage is one frontal MCU in front of the same building, repeated six times. Several beats the changelog says were fixed are not on screen. Noah floats in mid-air in s11d. The hug is still a wide shot in which the two figures merge (s17f). The film's thesis beat, where Noah chooses not to read a thought (s17e), shows no thought window in either still. The story now works and the school looks like the reference. The cold open, the exteriors and the acting do not yet.
