# Story-Flow audit — V2
SCORE: 7/10

VERDICT: V2 fixes most of the structural problems from V1 and is now a coherent film. The hook works. Anonymous "everyone" vignettes with readable thoughts give way to the MONDAY wake-up, and the café test proves the premise. The core question is stated by 9.9 s and the title lands at 29.0 s, matching the reference's 0:32–0:36 placement. Leo is planted five times before the conflict (s03a, s03b, s03d, s03h fist-bump, s05a2 high-five). The week is now visible (MONDAY chip, TUE/WED/THU montage, THURSDAY→FRIDAY flip). Act 2's second half has its own cool, uneasy grade. The early "HEAR ≠ UNDERSTAND" caption is gone. The realisation replays now happen in-world, and the key silences have room: 2.6 s after "He's gonna hate me", 3.9 s after "I KNOW what you're thinking", and a 3.6 s music-only hold before s16_vo1. The re-hook chain holds from start to finish, with no gap between questions longer than about 40 s. The film's thesis beat still doesn't work, though. In s17e the "unread" thought window is a tiny empty pill on screen for under 1 s, Noah's pupils never visibly flick to it, and Leo's "Thank god." plays fully on the soundtrack. In s18c Noah has his back to camera while "Please say yes." is fully audible. The audience hears exactly what Noah supposedly chose not to hear, so the ending still tells instead of showing. Contrast through progression is also only half-felt in the pacing. The cadence optimiser flattened the acts to 2.73 s / 3.25 s / 3.39 s mean shot length, and the "quiet" s15–s17 stretch is 19 cuts at a 3.5 s mean. The changelog also over-claims in several places I checked against the render: the cafeteria doors never open, the Friday courtyard isn't overcast, and s17a has no background thoughts. Runtime is 6:32 (391.998 s), still about 32 s over the 5–6 min target.
