# Logic / Sense audit — V2
SCORE: 6/10
VERDICT: V2 fixes most of what V1 broke at the level of single frames. Thought windows now sit on dark plates and can be read, and the important ones point at the right head: s08d's tail lands on Leo's cap, s12e is on Leo's side, s01g is beside the barista, and the s09f speculation comes out of Noah. Leo is planted in Act 1, Maya's reveal is clean, the letter is readable and in Noah's hands, and the week now has date chips. Several new contradictions came in with the fixes, though, and some of the changelog's claims are not on screen. The new 3:00 bell (s03i) ends the school day before the lunch scene, where a kid thinks "Two more periods". The "real sit-up" (s01d) has Noah sitting through the mattress, and in s11d Noah floats horizontally above his bed, which is the levitation problem from V1 again. In s08e Noah teleports about 15 m down the corridor and the screen axis flips. The cafeteria doors in s05b never open, because the cut was retimed to land before the door animation. The film's final lesson is that Noah still hears but chooses not to read. In s17e/s18c the audience hears "Thank god." and "Please say yes." out loud while the windows show "…" and "fade unread", and Noah's eye-flick isn't visible. A 12-year-old now follows the plot easily but would still ask "why is he floating?", "wasn't school over?", "how did Noah get over there?" and "did he hear it or not?".
