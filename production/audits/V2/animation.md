# Animation audit — V2
SCORE: 5/10

VERDICT: V2 is a real step up from V1, and several rig fixes are confirmed on screen. Front-view arms now fold to the chest instead of making a "W" (s01k clutch, s03g, s04e, s10c, s14e crossed). Seated characters sit on stools and benches (s02a2, s03h, s06d, s06e, s09b). Mouths blend with the emotion (open smile in s17d/s18d, and s16a turns from sad to a small smile). Leo's shoulder tap in s08c lands, the laptop slams shut in s09b, the barista's arm pushes the mug forward while Noah's hand comes into frame in s01f, and the letter stays in Noah's hand from s14d to s16a. Next to the Ifverse reference (contact sheets 01/04/06/08), the film still fails on **the big physical action beats**, and several of the CHANGELOG's prop claims are not true in the render. The cafeteria doors never open (s05b). The slow-motion mug hangs frozen in mid-air for the whole shot (s01i, a regression from V1). The high-five never connects (s05a2). The new `hipRot` puts Noah's legs through the mattress (s01d) and leaves him floating above his bed (s11d). The lesson-in-action beat in s17e lasts 0.3 s and can't be read. Breathing exists in code, but at 1.1 % h, below the ±1.5 % bible spec, it can't be seen in the strips, and many dialogue shots are still one held pose with only the mouth moving (s08a 5 s, s10d, s09a, s09d, s07d, s17b, s15c). Point and reach arms are still straight rods with no elbow bend. A viewer will still notice problems, so this sits at 5, not higher.

## Issues (most important first)
1. [s05b_doors] **The doors never open.** The 8-frame strip (114.1–116.8 s) shows Noah walking up and spreading his arms, with both door leaves shut and no light spill in any frame. Cause: `open = E.seg(t, E.M('doors'), E.M('doors')+0.6)` uses the absolute marker 117.149, which now falls after the retimed cut at 116.95. → FIX: key the action to local time: `open = E.seg(lt, D*0.55, D*0.8, 'out')`, with Noah's `push` blend at `D*0.4–0.55` so his palms touch the leaves first. Then add a 2-frame squash on contact and the light wedge. Also add `s05b_doors` to the optimiser's exempt list, or have `E.retime` refuse to move a cut before any `E.M()` marker the shot uses.

2. [s01i_slowmo, s01g_thought] **The slow-motion mug is frozen.** It hangs at the same x/y and angle for all 1.2 s (24.07 → 25.12), its lid floats above it, and Noah's reaching arm doesn't move. Only the pulse ring animates, so the "tumble, freeze, resume" never happens (V1's "what works" item has regressed). In s01g the slip is a small tilt in the last frames, and the mug is still in her hand at 23.00. → FIX: in s01i drive the mug with `y = y0 + 0.5·g·(ts)²`, `rot += 2.5·ts` at time scale 0.2, freeze it fully for 6 frames under the pulse, then let it fall out of the bottom of the frame by the end. Animate Noah's arm reaching forward 0→0.3 rad. In s01g start the slip at 60 % of the shot: the mug slides 30 px down her fingers and rotates 25°, her fingers open (forearm drops 0.2 rad), and her smile holds (thought rule).

[s01d_wake, s11d_date] **`hipRot` bed bugs.** In s01d (9.6–10.6 s) the lying pose uses `pose:'sit'` rotated −90°, so the thighs point straight up and Noah lies with his knees in the air above the duvet. After the sit-up (11.3–12.0 s) the shins hang vertically through the mattress to the floor. In s11d (`y:700`, `pose:'stand'`) the hip pivot sits at y≈538 while the mattress top is at about 660, so Noah floats above the bed with his legs behind the date card, motionless for 3 s. → FIX: add a `sitBed` pose (legs straight forward, `lB/lF ≈ [π/2, 0]` relative to the torso) and use it for both shots. s01d: anchor the hip on the mattress top (`hipY = 655`) with the head starting on the pillow, and keep the duvet over the legs. s11d: set `y = 660 + 0.45*h` and `x = pillowX + 0.5*h`, move the date card to the upper right, and add the toss-turn (f 0.3→−0.3 at 50 %) and a blink on the flip.

4. [s05a2_highfive] **The high-five never connects.** Both boys stop walking at about 111.1 s and raise one arm straight up in front of their own faces, which hides Noah's face. Use a high-five arm aimed at the meeting point (`aF` about −2.3 rad with a 0.3 elbow bend, the hand ending at the x-midpoint of the two characters at head-top height), drawn on the camera side of the head. Put the palms in contact for 3 frames at about 40 %, with a ±4 px recoil and the glow centred on the contact point. Add a 2-frame anticipation (arm drawn back) before the slap.
