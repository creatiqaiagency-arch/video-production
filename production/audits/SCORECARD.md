## V1 → V2: what the team asked for (condensed)
- **Perspective & coverage**: sets were flat stages; add vanishing-point sets, reverse angles, high/low/top-down setups; rebuild Act 3 courtyard on a second axis.
- **Lighting**: key-side-only rims in key colour, clothing shading, un-blown windows/skies, three-state Act 3 light (confrontation → reveal → reconciliation), time-of-day arc (morning Act 1 → afternoon → dusk → night → Friday → sunset).
- **Animation**: fix front-view arm bug, breathing/sway, more poses per line, seats under sitting characters, hip-pivot sit-up, props that move (cup, doors, letter, phone).
- **Readability**: thought windows on dark backing, inside safe area, on the right head, fully revealed ≥1 s.
- **Story**: plant Leo in Act 1, show the week passing (MON chip + TUE/WED/THU montage), lesson shown through action at the end, Noah apologises to Maya, give silences room, remove early "HEAR ≠ UNDERSTAND" caption, memory replays in-world.
- **Cadence**: split >5 s shots on VO pauses, merge sub-1.5 s shots, cut on phrase boundaries.

## V2 → V3: what the team asked for (condensed, full list in `V3/BRIEF.md`)
- **First 30 s**: readable teaser thoughts, bed sit-up fixed, crash on a real floor, opaque title, true morning.
- **Perspective everywhere**: café, street, library, bedroom, courtyard building; real high/low/Dutch/profile angles.
- **Act 3**: vary Noah's coverage (profile, low, OTS, ECU), slower moving holds, MS hug, silhouettes walking into the sun.
- **The thesis beat** (s17e/s18): readable forming thought, visible eye/head flick, the thought audio cut off unread.
- **Light**: truly cool overcast confrontation → warm gold with cool fill; less lavender thought windows; corridor light pool.
- **Cadence**: too many shots for the runtime (metronome at 3.0 s, mid-phrase cuts); merge shots, honour phrase boundaries, beats 1–2.5 s.
- **Fix the false changelog claims**: doors opening, Friday overcast, morning café, phone at ear, letter from bag, clock snap.
