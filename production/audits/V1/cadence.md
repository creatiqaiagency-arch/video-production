# Shot-Cadence audit — V1
SCORE: 5/10
VERDICT: On raw numbers V1 is close to the reference: 112 shots, mean 3.47 s and median 3.30 s against the reference's 3.1 s median, and nothing is held longer than 6.6 s. Two things hold it back. First, the client rule is broken often: only 49/112 shots (44%) fall in 3–5 s, and 10 shots in Acts 1–2 run over 5 s, where no exception applies (s02a, s02b, s02c, s05a, s06f, s07a, s09c, s09f, s10d, s10g). In Act 3, 5 of the 6 shots over 5 s are locked-off (s12a, s14d, s15b, s16a, s17e), which the "emotional hold" exception does not allow; s13a, which tracks, is the only one that qualifies. Five shots run under 1.1 s, and there are runs of 4–5 shots under 3 s (s13b–s13f, s01g–s01j, s03f–s03i). 20 cuts land inside a spoken phrase, and some clip the last word (s13e "Leo!", s14b "What?", s18d "that"). Second, and this is what a viewer will notice most: the film has much less angle variety than the reference. The Ifverse contact sheets change camera height and axis on almost every composition (top-down, low, 3/4, OTS, Dutch, foreground framing). V1 is shot almost entirely flat, frontal, eye-level and parallel to one backdrop. The Act 3 courtyard conversation (s13c–s17e) cuts back and forth between the same two frontal close-ups of Noah and Leo, against the same blurred building wall, about 15 times. The hallway scenes s08–s10 reuse the same side-on locker two-shot. The cut rhythm is also inverted across acts: Act 2 averages 3.71 s against a 2.5–3.5 s target, and Act 3 averages 3.49 s against 4–6 s. Act 3 is cut faster than the overload act and with less camera movement.

Per-act numbers: Act 1 (S01–S05) 38 shots, mean 3.23 s, 39% in 3–5 s. Act 2 (S06–S11) 34 shots, mean 3.71 s, 44%. Act 3 (S12–S18) 40 shots, mean 3.49 s, 48%.

## What works (keep)
- Overall density matches the reference: a 3.3 s median cut, nothing static for 7 s or more, and no dead air between scenes.
- Cuts land well where the edit is audio-led: s03c→s03d→s03e ("Uh…" then the B), s04f/s04g/s04h (No way / Why is he smiling / Oh), s08d→s08e and s12c→s12d→s12e are all within about 0.1–0.4 s of a line ending.
- The silence after emotional lines follows the style bible: s08f "He's gonna hate me" gets 1.8 s before the cut and s15f gets 1.8 s. Keep these.
- s04b→s04c→s04d gives real variety: a one-point corridor reveal, a comedic locker hide, and a long-lens push from behind Noah. Use it as the template for the other hallway scenes.
- s05c→s05d→s06a→s06b (OTS → push into the panels → orbit → top-down overhead) is the most reference-like run in the film. The angle changes on every cut and the shot sizes escalate.
- s12d/s12e is a correct OTS shot/reverse pair, and s13a's tracking two-shot correctly uses the moving-camera allowance for a 6.6 s hold.
- s15e_memory is a good short cutaway that breaks the courtyard background; more of this kind of shot is needed in Act 3.
