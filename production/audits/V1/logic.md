# Logic / Sense audit — V1
SCORE: 5/10
VERDICT: The story logic from the screenplay mostly survives. Act 3 holds a consistent screen axis (Noah left, Leo right), the speculation panels are clearly marked as "not real", the overlays vanish on Maya's "...What?", and the SAID / THOUGHT / MEANT revisit pays the theme off cleanly. The film's own premise breaks down on screen, though. Roughly a dozen of the thoughts Noah "hears" can't be read: windows sit on top of bright background windows and wash out to white, get covered by scenery, or clip their text. Several thoughts are attached to the wrong head or drawn in the wrong visual language, such as Leo's first painful thought rendered as white caption text beside Noah, and Leo's worries written as chalk on a blackboard. Props the narration mentions are missing (the phone in s09c, Leo's thought window in s11c). The opening cup gag has no visible cause, and the paper cup shatters like ceramic. All of Act 1 is lit as dusk although the narrator says "one morning". A 12-year-old would follow the broad story but would often ask "who thought that?" and "what does that window say?". This version is watchable but not yet clear.

## Issues (most important first)
1. [s01f, s01j, s02d, s04e, s04g, s08f, s09d, s10b, s10d, s14e, s16b–d, s18c] PROBLEM: Thought text can't be read. Windows are placed over the bright yellow background windows, so the 18 % violet fill turns white and the text disappears (s04g "Why is h…", s10b "He s…", s14e "…e just be", s18c is a blank white pill, and the pink THOUGHT boxes in s16b–d are unreadable). Text also overflows the window (s01f "…Please don't spill" is clipped at the right edge, and s02d's text runs under the oven icon). → FIX: In the thought-window renderer, add a dark backing plate (e.g. `#1c1430` at 65–75 %) under the 18 % violet fill. Auto-wrap and auto-size the text so it always fits inside the rect (measureText, then wrap and grow the height). Enforce the bible's 44–64 px size. Add a placement pass that nudges each window onto a dark wall or locker area and never over a lit window rect. Re-check each listed shot.
2. [s08d] PROBLEM: Leo's "I can't keep doing this." is drawn as plain white text right next to NOAH's head, with no window, no tail and Leo out of frame. By our grammar, white text means spoken dialogue, so it reads as Noah talking or thinking. This is the most important thought in the film. → FIX: Render it as a standard violet thought window with its tail dots running off-frame toward screen-right, where Leo stands in s08c. Better, keep Leo's blurred shoulder or cap in the right foreground so the tail visibly lands on his head. Keep the dark background, and keep the word-by-word reveal inside the window.
3. [s12e, s01f, s09f] PROBLEM: Windows sit next to the wrong head. In s12e, Leo's "Oh no. Not like this." sits against Noah's foreground head and its first words are cut off behind his silhouette. In s01f, the barista's window floats next to Noah's head at the right edge. In s09f, a grey speculation panel sits directly above Leo's head, so Noah's imagined fear reads as Leo's real thought. → FIX: s12e: move the window to the right of Leo's head (frame x ≈ 0.6–0.85, above his cap) and draw it above Noah's silhouette layer. s01f: move the window to the left or above the barista's bun, at least 20 % of frame width away from Noah's head. s09f: anchor all speculation panels above or around Noah (left half of frame) and keep them clear of Leo, so the torn panels visibly come out of Noah.
4. [s09a] PROBLEM: Leo's echoed thoughts appear as white handwriting on the classroom chalkboard, so it looks as if someone wrote "He's gonna hate me" on the board. → FIX: Render the echoes as faded, smaller violet thought windows (or ghost text with violet glow) drifting around Noah's head, in front of the chalkboard and not on it. Use a lower alpha (≈ 40 %) so they read as memory, not a live thought.
5. [s09c] PROBLEM: The shot is "Leo laughs on the phone, sees Noah, hangs up abruptly", but there is no phone. Leo just stands with his arm down. Narration ("Had he been lying this whole time?") then has nothing to point at. → FIX: Draw a phone in Leo's hand raised to his ear with a laughing mouth for the first ~60 % of the shot. Then a quick lower-and-pocket move with an awkward smile when he notices Noah. Optionally add a small phone-screen glow.
6. [s01f → s01i] PROBLEM: The opening gag's cause and effect is unclear, and the props are inconsistent. We never see the cup slip; in s01h it is already tumbling in mid-air. The s01h foreground silhouette has a bun (the barista) on the LEFT, whereas Noah's head was on the RIGHT in s01e/f, so screen direction flips and the viewer can't tell whose shoulder it is. The cup is a takeaway paper cup with a lid, yet in s01i it smashes into ceramic shards. The s01g "eyes" are two beige circles that don't read as Noah's eyes, and their reflections show the barista holding the cup, not the cup falling. → FIX: Make the last ~0.4 s of s01f show the cup sliding out of her fingers. In s01h, put Noah's spiky silhouette in the right foreground, matching s01e. Swap the paper cup for a ceramic mug in s01e/f/h, as in the reference café, so the shatter makes sense (or keep paper and make s01i a lid-pop plus splash, with no shards). In s01g, draw Noah's black face with white eye shapes and put a tiny falling-mug reflection in each pupil.
7. He floats fully in mid-air above the bed inside a glowing ring, so it reads as a levitation or flying power, which misleads viewers about what his power is. → FIX: Keep his hips on the mattress and animate a torso pivot up to a sitting pose. Put the violet shimmer as a ring or pulse around his head only. Don't move his feet off the bed.
9. [s11c, s11d] PROBLEM: Leo's "Tomorrow's going to change everything." has no thought window at all (audio only). - Mouth-closed rule for thoughts is mostly respected: barista s01f, Maya s04e/s04g/s10b, Leo s08f/s09e/s14e, crowd s06d. Spoken lines have open mouths (s01e, s03c, s08c, s10a, s18b).

- s10d → s10e: every overlay vanishes on "Then don't say ANYTHING!" and Maya's hurt "...What?" plays in a clean frame. The beat lands.
- Act 3 screen direction is solid: Noah screen-left and Leo screen-right through s12a–s17f, including correct reverse OTS pairs (s12d/s12e, s14a).
- Costume continuity is strong: Noah's red hoodie and Leo's green cap and teal shirt are consistent everywhere, including the age-8 photo (s08b) and the memory (s15e).

- s16b–d SAID / THOUGHT / MEANT revisit cards make the theme explicit for a young viewer (once the thought line is legible).
- s17e: Leo's "Thank god" is small and soft, which fits the rule that Noah stops reacting. s18 Maya enters from the left as scripted.
