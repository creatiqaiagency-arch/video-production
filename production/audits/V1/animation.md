# Animation audit — V1
SCORE: 4/10

VERDICT: V1 has working basics. Walk cycles read, lip-flap follows the VO envelope, thought lines never move a mouth, blinks exist, pose changes go through `poseAt` with easing, and the cup fall and shatter (s01h/s01i) works. Next to the Ifverse reference (contact sheets 01/04/06/08), the acting is far behind. The reference uses three-quarter angles, bent limbs, hands that touch props and people, characters sitting on real chairs and full-body reactions. V1 mostly shows front-facing figures holding one pose for the whole shot. The biggest problem is a **rig bug in front-facing shots**. When a character faces the camera (|f| ≤ 0.35, so `fd = 0`), every "forward" arm angle splays outward. As a result, `clutch` (hands to chest), `crossed`, `letter` and `hug` all render as the same "W" surrender/shrug pose. That one bug breaks the acting in at least 12 close-ups, and the scripted action ("hands to chest", "arms crossed", "looks down at the letter in his hands") is visibly not happening. Other problems: characters sit on nothing, Noah floats above his bed in s01d, idle breathing is too weak to see (head-only, ±0.6 %), the talking mouth is always the same "O" whatever the emotion, and several key props are missing, teleport or are timed wrongly (phone, coffee hand-off, letter). A viewer will notice. Fix the rig and the breathing first, because those two code changes lift almost every shot.

## Issues (most important first)

1. [s01j, s10c, s10g, s03g, s14e, s10e, s05d, s03d, s15c, s16e, s05b] **The front-view rig mirrors "forward" arms outward.** In `character.js` → `limb()`, `dir = fd !== 0 ? fd : side`. When `fd = 0`, `clutch`, `crossed`, `letter` and `hug` bend away from the body, so all of them come out as the same "W" shrug. s01j's barista should have her hands at her chest (code: `clutch`) and does a surrender pose. In s10c Noah should have his arms crossed (code: `crossed`) and does a W. In s15c/s16e the `letter` pose spreads the hands wide, so `PROP.letter`, placed at the midpoint between the hands, ends up pinned flat to his chest with no hand touching it. In s05b the arms stick straight out in a T-pose. → FIX: give each pose a front-view mode. Add `front:'in'` to `clutch, crossed, letter, hug, phone, laptop, sitDesk, holdOut, bag, facepalm`. In `limb()`, when `fd===0 && pose.front==='in'`, use `dir = -side` for the forearm bend (upper arm stays slightly outward, `a1 = ang*0.4*side`, `a2 = a1 - bend*side`), so the hands meet in front of the chest. Check by rendering each POSE at f=0 on one test frame before re-rendering shots. For the letter, draw the prop only when both hands are within 0.25·h of each other; otherwise attach it to `hf`.

2. [all dialogue CUs, especially s15b (6.2 s), s16a (6.5 s), s14d (6.4 s), s13e, s01j, s16f, s04b, s12a] **Characters are statues.** Each shot uses one pose for its whole length, and only the mouth moves. Idle `bob` = `0.006*h` and only moves the head, which is below the style bible's ±1.5 % torso/head breathing and can't be seen in the strips (s15c, s13e and s16f frames 1–8 look identical apart from the mouth). → FIX: (a) In `E.char`, apply breathing to the shoulders and chest as well: `breath = sin(t*1.6+seed)*0.015*h`, add it to `shY`/`neckY`/`headY`, add a 0.5× shoulder rise, and add an arm micro-sway of `±0.04 rad` at 0.3 Hz to `aB/aF[0]`. (b) Give every spoken line 2–4 key poses through `poseAt`, plus a head-tilt change (`tilt` ±0.08) on stressed words. Examples: s15b Leo `[[0,'pocket'],[1.8,'clutch'],[4.2,'relaxed']]`, look down on "pretending" then up on "leaving". s13e Noah anticipation (`lean -0.1` for 3 frames), then `point` with ease-out-back on "KNOW", relaxing to `relaxed` at 70 %. s16f: Noah lowers the letter and turns `f` from 0.8 to 0.5 toward Leo.

3. [s01d_wake] **The sit-up pivots from the feet, so Noah floats diagonally above the bed** (13.8 s still: body detached in mid-air, legs dangling over the duvet). `E.char` rotates around its origin, which is the feet. → FIX: pivot from the hips. Keep a fixed hip point on the mattress (e.g. `hip=[300,680]`). Each frame, set `x = hip.x - sin(rot)*0.45*h` and `y = hip.y + cos(rot)*0.45*h` with `rot` going from -π/2 to 0 (fully upright), and use pose `sit` so the legs stay horizontal under the duvet. Keep the duvet drawn over the legs. Add a 3-frame anticipation (head dips back) before the sit-up, and an ease-out-back overshoot on the eyes opening.

Put the phone on the camera-side hand (flip to `f:-0.6` or swap the prop to `hb`). ## What works (keep)
- **Walk cycles** (s02b, s04a, s05a strut, s09f, s10f, s12b, s15e, s18e): clear leg swing and counter-swing that read well at every distance.
- **Thought vs speech mouth rule**: thoughts keep the mouth closed (s01f barista smiling through "Please don't spill it", s04e, s06d/e, s08f, s18c), and only the speaker flaps, driven by the VO envelope (`E.talk` excludes thoughts).
- **Snappy expression beats**: s04f "No way" → fist-pump cheer, s03f hand shoots up with a happy squint, s04h freeze to an awkward flat mouth, s17a wide-eyes → grin, s13d shock eyes.
- **Effects animation**: bubbles pop in and multiply (s05c, steadily rising motion in the strip), the s06b counter ramps up, thought text types on word by word (s08d, s08f), and the streaming thought-lines in s07a pass through the head.
- **Physics prop**: the s01h slow-mo tumble in the violet pulse ring and the s01i shard scatter plus spreading puddle follow the style bible.
- **Head-clutch / overload poses** (s02a, s06f, s07a, s06g): a readable silhouette that sells the overwhelm.
- Blinks implemented with per-character seeds; pose transitions use eased `poseAt` blending.
