# Story-Flow audit — V1
SCORE: 6/10

VERDICT: V1 follows the client script faithfully and the macro-structure works. The cold open proves the premise with a physical event (falling cup). The title lands at 26.5 s, close to the reference's 0:32, over the same kind of café-façade composition. The re-hook chain mostly holds: "is it real?" (0:31) → "what can he do with it?" (0:58) → "what does Maya think?" (1:22) → "until the cafeteria" (1:47) → "can he escape?" (2:29) → "what can't Leo keep doing?" (2:56) → "what happens Friday?" (3:56) → the scholarship reveal (4:44). No gap between new questions runs longer than about 40 s. The film still falls short of the reference in three places. (1) The lesson is narrated and captioned but never acted: Noah never visibly chooses to ignore a thought, so the ending tells instead of showing. (2) Contrast through progression only works in the middle. The cafeteria overload (S5–S7) escalates well. But Act 1, the second half of Act 2 and Act 3 all share the same pink-dusk grade, the same warm hallway and similar cutting rhythms. Act 2's mean shot (3.71 s) is actually longer than Act 1's (3.23 s), and the "quiet" Act 3 is cut choppily (1.5–1.8 s shots). (3) The film rushes key emotional turns ("...What?" lasts 0.67 s, the post-confession silence 2.4 s, the music-in before the realization VO 2.0 s). Meanwhile, structural gaps confuse the timeline: four days pass invisibly, Leo first appears at 2:53 with no Act-1 presence, and Noah's snap at Maya is never repaired before she asks him to walk home. Runtime is 6:29 (6:24 before the end card), about 25 s over the 5–6 min target.

## Issues (most important first)
1. [s17a_dude, s17e_thankgod, s18c_sayyes, s18d_yes] The ending doesn't demonstrate the lesson through action. The shot list promises "faint thought windows linger… he ignores them", but no windows are visible in s17a. In s17e Leo's "Thank god." appears while only Leo is in frame, so Noah's not-reacting is invisible. In s18c "Please say yes" is an unreadable white smear and Noah is again off-screen. The film's whole thesis rests on a choice the viewer never sees Noah make. → FIX: Restage s17e as a two-shot (Noah left, Leo right). As Leo laughs, a small violet thought window starts to pop beside Leo's head. Noah's eyes flick to it for 6–8 frames, then he deliberately turns his eyes and head back to Leo's face and smiles. The window fades out unread over 0.5 s, with its text never resolving; keep the "Thank god." audio very low or drop it. Repeat the same gesture in s18c/s18d: Maya's thought begins to form and Noah looks at her eyes, not the window, then says "Yeah. I'd like that." Hold s18d at 3.0 s instead of 2.13 s.

2. [s09f_speculation → s10a_okay → s11a_clock; s13a_track "all week"] The passage of days is invisible. Everything from S1 to S10 plays as one Monday ("I hate Mondays", "by the end of the day"). Then s11a cuts to a clock that looks identical to the Monday clock in s03i, and the film suddenly says it is Thursday. The viewer never feels the week of suspicion that Noah's "you've been hiding something from me all week" depends on. → FIX: Turn s11a_clock (4.0 s) into a day-flip montage of 4 × 1.0 s cuts, each with a corner date chip in the s11d_date style: "TUE" Leo turns away from Noah at the lockers; "WED" Leo eats alone with his phone; "THU" the clock nears 3:00. Keep the s11 VO on top. Also add a "MONDAY" chip to s01d_wake so the countdown starts on screen.

3. [s08c_twoshot, s03a–s03h, s05a_lucky, s04a_hall, s04b_maya_reveal] Leo isn't set up. The "best friend since they were kids" first appears at 2:53, nearly halfway through. The stakes of Act 3 depend on a friendship the audience has never seen. Worse, green/cyan-capped extras in s04a (blurred foreground) and s04b (beside Maya) read as Leo before his introduction. → FIX: Plant Leo twice in Act 1: (a) in s03b_wide, seat Leo (green cap) at the desk next to Noah; on s03g_correct he gives Noah a stunned look, and on the s03h montage the two fist-bump. (b) In s05a_lucky, Leo walks past and high-fives Noah's strut. Recolour every extra's cap in s04a/s04b to anything but green.

4. [s14b_what, s13f_distance, s15f_first → s16a_face, s08f_hateme → s09a_stare] The key emotional turns are rushed. Noah's "...What?" (the film's pivot from anger to shock) lasts 0.67 s. The scripted [Silence.] after "I KNOW what you're thinking" gets a 2.4 s wide. The narrator starts 2.0 s after "I wanted you to be the first person I told." Only 1.7 s separates "He's gonna hate me" from the next scene. → FIX: Extend s14b_what to 2.0 s, with the brows relaxing and the arm dropping during the hold. Extend s13f_distance to 3.5 s with a full audio dropout: no bed, only wind, starting 0.3 s after the line. After s15f, hold a silent 3.0 s on Noah's face, starting s16a's picture before the VO and letting the music swell alone, so s16_vo1 starts at least 3.5 s after s15_leo3 ends. Add 1.0 s of silent hold to the end of s08f_hateme.

5. [s16a_face, s16b_maya, s16c_teacher, s16d_leo, s16e_hear] The realization is told rather than shown, and it breaks the visual arc. s14e ends with "violet overlays fade out of the film here", yet s16b–d bring back large purple glass UI panels with "SAID / THOUGHT / MEANT" captions that spell out the moral ("She cared about him."). Meanwhile s16a is a 6.5 s near-static face under a 19.4 s narrator block, the longest VO in the film, inside the act that is supposed to be quiet. → FIX: Rebuild s16b–d as "memory replays" of the original shots (the s10d Maya OTS, the s03c teacher, the s08c Leo two-shot). Re-use those backgrounds in a warm, desaturated, soft-vignette grade, with the spoken line and the thought both shown as small subtitles side by side. 6. [s10g_understand] The moral is captioned 1:50 too early. The on-screen "HEAR ≠ UNDERSTAND" labels state the film's thesis at 3:50, which takes the punch out of S16. The VO line is in the script, but the caption isn't. → FIX: Remove the HEAR / UNDERSTAND / ≠ text from s10g. Keep the shot on Noah looking down at his hands and add a slow 5 % push-in. Let the VO carry it.

7. [s01a–s01k, s02b_outside, s12a–s17f, s18a–s18e] Time of day contradicts the story, and the act contrast is lost. The VO says "one morning", but the café (s01a–s01k) and the "morning street" (s02b) are pink dusk with sun rays. The Friday courtyard (S12–S17) has lit windows and an evening sky, so it reads as the same dusk as Thursday's s11b, and S18's "late-afternoon" sunset is barely different. The client's "contrast through progression" has no time-of-day or colour arc to ride on. → FIX: Give the film a time-of-day arc. Act 1 (S1–S5): bright cool-warm morning, with sun high, no sunset rays and unlit café windows. Act 2 hallway scenes (S8–S10): flatter, cooler afternoon. S11b: dusk. S11d: night. S12–S14 Friday courtyard: overcast, desaturated morning with unlit windows. Let it warm gradually through S15–S17 (add a sun break at s15e/s16f). S18: the only true golden sunset in the film, so the final walk-off feels earned.

8. [s08a_locker → s11d_date] The second half of Act 2 loses its discomfort. After the excellent cafeteria spiral, S8–S10 return to the exact warm Act-1 hallway (same lockers, glowing windows and corkboard as the playful s04 Maya scene), at a slower cut rhythm. The paranoia beats look like the crush beats. → FIX: For S9–S10, re-grade the hallway cooler and darker (windows dimmer, lockers desaturated). Keep a faint, low-opacity layer of drifting thought-noise at frame edges around Noah in every S9–S10 shot; the script calls for "harsh purple noise", which is barely present in s10a. Frame Noah tighter and slightly off-centre, with more negative space. Trim s09c_phone from 5.84 s to 4.0 s, giving the time to an added insert (see issue 9).

9. [s09b_laptop, s09c_phone] Noah's paranoia has no visible cause. In s09b the laptop is an unreadable white line, and in s09c Leo has no phone, so the "ends a call abruptly" beat doesn't exist. The narrator asks "was Leo lying?" over evidence the viewer can't see. → FIX: s09b: give the laptop a clearly visible open screen that Leo slams shut. On its last open frame, show a blurred crest with the Ashford "A" logo from s14c (a plant that pays off at the reveal). s09c: put a phone at Leo's ear laughing, then have him snap it down and pocket it with an awkward smile when Noah enters. Add a 1.0 s insert of the phone screen going dark on "Mom".

## What works (keep)
- Cold open: smile/thought contradiction → ECU → freeze → crash → "I'm getting fired" (s01e–s01j) is a clean premise-proof gag. The title card at 26.5 s over the café façade (s01k) matches the reference's structure and placement.
- The re-hook chain is solid: every scene from S2 to S14 raises or answers a question, and the scene-ending VO lines ("Until he walked into the school cafeteria", "What was Leo planning to tell him on Friday?") hand off cleanly.
- The S5–S7 overload escalation: cafeteria doors from behind (s05b) → thought shapes multiply (s05c) → infinite violet space (s06a) → "199 THOUGHTS" counter (s06b) → hard cut to black (s06g). This is the strongest stretch of the film.
- s07a/s07b on-screen "NOTHING." / "STILL NOTHING." and the bathroom wide kill viewer objections exactly as the style guide asks.
- s08a/s08b locker photo of Noah and Leo at age 8 sets up s15e's childhood memory: a real setup/payoff (keep it; give s15e 3.0 s instead of 2.3 s).
- s09f's grey torn speculation panels clearly separate imagined from heard thoughts.
- In s10d/s10e every overlay vanishes when Noah snaps, and Maya's hurt is held on a clean frame. Correct and affecting.
- s12a's extreme-wide empty courtyard, bookended by the s17f hug pull-out to the same composition, gives Act 3 a frame.
- The s14c acceptance-letter insert is specific and readable and lands the reveal.
- The end card's "What would YOU do…?" with the cast line-up mirrors the reference CTA format; the 5 s length is right.
