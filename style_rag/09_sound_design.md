# 09 — Sound design

## Voice
- Narrator: warm, clear American male storyteller, conversational, smiling on jokes, slower on emotional beats.
- Characters: young, expressive, short lines. Character lines sit slightly lower in the mix than narration but are drier/closer.
- Generated with **ElevenLabs** (`eleven_v3`, inline audio tags like `[whispers]`, `[shouting]`).
  - Narrator: *Brad – Warm, Trusted Storyteller*
  - Leo: *VALF – Anime Protagonist Charismatic*
  - Nora: *Kathie – Sassy & Playful*
  - Sam: *Surfer McDude*

## SFX palette (every on-screen action needs a sound)
whooshes on camera moves · heartbeat thumps on tension · digital shimmer/chime on preview activation · reversed impact on future-snap-back · glass scrape/catch · coin clicks (they become the beat) · paddle "tok" · shoe squeaks · arcade beeps · crowd/café murmur · city traffic bed · truck engine + air-brake hiss + tyre skid · heavy crash with debris · ringing tinnitus during previews.

## Music
Light, curious plucked/marimba motif in Act I–II; muted pulse/synth drone in Act III; percussive tension rising in Act IV; silence at the crash; a single soft resolving note at the end.

## Mix
VO −16 LUFS-ish dominant; music ducked −12 dB under VO; SFX punchy but under VO except the crash. During future previews, low-pass the ambience (muffled) and add a high ring; restore full sound on Leo's shout.
