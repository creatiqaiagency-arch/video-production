# 08 — Typography & graphics

## Title card (reference 0:36)
- Two lines, centred over the establishing shot: line 1 smaller (`WHAT IF YOUR REACTION TIME`),
  line 2 larger (`WAS 10x FASTER?`).
- Heavy rounded geometric sans, ALL CAPS, wide letter-spacing (~0.12 em).
  We use **"Nunito" 900** (fallback: Arial Black). Colour `#ffefd7`, outer glow warm `#ffb070`
  at 40 %, soft dark drop shadow.
- Animates in letter-by-letter or with a soft scale 0.96 → 1.0 + fade, holds ~3 s.

## Labels / data overlays (reference 0:45, 1:03, 2:48)
- Small caps labels (`EYES → BRAIN → MUSCLES`, `NORMAL: 30 cm / YOU: 3 mm`), 26–34 px,
  letter-spaced, cream with glow; numbers in bold.
- Thin cream lines/arrows connecting labels to objects. Appear with a wipe.

## End screen (reference 7:03)
- Italic serif question `Which sport would YOU dominate?` with the key word in yellow, cast line-up,
  red Subscribe pill. For us: *"What would YOU do if you could hear everyone's thoughts?"*

## Thought graphics (project-specific — see `12_thought_visual_language.md`)
- Thought text: **handwritten-feel rounded font** ("Caveat" / "Patrick Hand"), violet glow.
- Thought windows: rounded rectangles, translucent violet fill (15–25 %), 2 px glowing border,
  tiny tail dots pointing to the thinker's head.
