# 03 — Characters

Source: `key_117s.jpg` (crowd + rim light), contact sheets 01 (café), 03 (bedroom), 06 (crouch).

## The rig (identical for every character)
- **Head**: perfect circle, body colour `#0a080d`. Head diameter ≈ 0.28 × standing height.
- **Neck/torso**: a single thick rounded stroke (or the garment shape) — no anatomy.
- **Limbs**: rounded-cap strokes, width ≈ 0.11 × head diameter… thick and soft. Two segments
  each (upper/lower) so elbows and knees bend. Hands are small rounded mitts (sometimes 4-finger
  silhouettes in close-ups). Feet are short rounded capsules.
- **Garment** sits *on top* of the body: hoodie = rounded trapezoid torso + sleeves (garment
  colour) + hood lump behind the neck + two white drawstrings; caps = half-dome + brim.
- **Proportions**: adults ≈ 1.0, teens ≈ 0.92, kids ≈ 0.7. Long legs (≈ 45 % of height).

## Face (white marks only, `#f4ede6`)
| Expression | Eyes | Mouth | Brows |
|---|---|---|---|
| neutral | two small dots | none or short line | none |
| happy | upside-down arcs `^ ^` | wide smile arc | — |
| smug | half-lidded (dot + lid line) | lopsided smile | one raised |
| shocked | big rings with dot pupils | small "O" | raised |
| worried | dots | wavy line `~` | slanted inward-up |
| angry | dots | flat/frown | slanted down-in |
| sad | dots looking down | small frown | inner ends up |
| squint (pain) | `> <` | gritted line | — |
| awkward grin | dots looking away | tight wide flat grin | raised |
Face marks sit on the side the head is turned toward (3/4 view = marks shifted ~25 % of radius).
Blink every 2–5 s (2-frame close). Talking = mouth shape cycles open/closed driven by VO loudness.

## Rim light (signature!)
Draw the whole silhouette first in the rim colour, offset 3–8 px (scaled) **toward the key light**,
then the black character on top. Result: a crisp warm sliver on the lit side. In night scenes use
the cool rim `#9fc6ff`. In close-ups the rim is 6–12 px. Background extras get a dimmer rim.
