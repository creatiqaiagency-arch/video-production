# 03 — Character design

## Construction (all characters)
Units are in "head diameters" (H). Adult H ≈ 1/7.5 of standing height.

| Part | Spec |
|---|---|
| Head | Perfect circle, diameter H. |
| Neck | Short thick stroke, width 0.28 H, length 0.25 H. |
| Torso | Thick rounded stroke from neck base to hip, width 0.42 H, length 2.0 H. Clothing drawn as a rounded rectangle/path over it. |
| Arms | Two segments (upper 1.15 H, fore 1.1 H), width 0.24 H, round caps. Hands = small circle (0.3 H) or mitten shape for close-ups. |
| Legs | Two segments (thigh 1.5 H, shin 1.5 H), width 0.26 H. Feet = short rounded stroke pointing forward (0.45 H). |
| Colour | Body `#0b0709`. Clothing flat colour with one darker shade for the far-side limb. |
| Rim | Duplicate silhouette, offset 3–6 px toward the key light, filled with rim colour, drawn behind the body. |

Children are ~0.6× adult height with a relatively bigger head (H = 1/5.5 of height).

## Faces (white `#f6f1e8` only)
| Expression | Eyes | Mouth | Brows |
|---|---|---|---|
| neutral | two vertical ovals | short flat line | none |
| happy | upside-down arcs (^ ^) | wide smile arc | none |
| smug | half-closed (flat top lids) | asymmetric smirk | one raised |
| shocked | big round circles with small pupils | small "o" | raised arcs |
| scared | big circles, pupils shrink, sweat drop | wobbly open | raised, slanted inward |
| angry/focused | narrowed (flat top) | flat/teeth line | slanted down to centre |
| sad | ovals, looking down | small downturned arc | slanted up to centre |
| talking | any eyes | mouth alternates between 3 shapes (closed / mid / open oval) at ~10 fps synced to VO amplitude |

Face sits on the side of the head the character faces (3/4 view): eyes shift ~0.18 H toward facing direction.
Blink every 2–5 s (2 frames closed). Eyes catch a tiny sparkle in extreme close-ups.

## Consistency rules
- Same outfit and colour in every scene unless the story says otherwise.
- Head size never changes within a shot (camera zoom is a camera move, not a scale change).
- Never let two characters overlap in a way that merges silhouettes; offset depth with blur or rim.
