# 01 — Art style

Source: contact sheets 01–09, key frames `key_036s.jpg`, `key_117s.jpg`, `key_381s.jpg`.

## Rendering technique
- **Flat vector shapes with smooth gradients**, no outlines on environments. Edges are defined by
  value contrast, not strokes. (It reads like After Effects / vector illustration composited in 2.5D.)
- **Characters are the only near-black objects** in frame. Environments sit in the mid-dark plum
  range so the silhouette always pops.
- Large shapes get **soft vertical gradients** (sky, walls, floors) — lighter toward the light source.
- Small details are sparse but specific: window mullions, awning scallops, lamp shades, a cup
  icon sign, potted flowers, railings, street lamps. "Few details, chosen well."
- Surfaces are matte. Only glass (windows, screens, glasses lenses) and the ball get highlights.
- **Atmospheric perspective**: far layers fade toward the sky colour and lose contrast.

## Shape language
- Characters: circles (heads) + rounded capsules (limbs). Friendly, readable at any size.
- Architecture: tall rectangles, grids of windows, horizontal bands (awnings, ledges, kerbs).
- Nature: trees are lollipop silhouettes (dark circular canopy clusters on a thin trunk).
- Props are simplified to 2–4 shapes (mug = trapezoid + handle ring + rim ellipse).

## Texture layer (applied to every frame)
- Fine animated **film grain** (monochrome, ~4–6 % opacity).
- **Floating dust motes / embers** drifting slowly in lit areas.
- **Bokeh discs** (soft circles, 8–15 % opacity) in out-of-focus foregrounds/backgrounds.
- Subtle **vignette** (corners ~35 % darker).

## Scale & staging
- Wide shots make characters tiny (5–10 % of frame height) inside big architecture.
- Close-ups fill the frame with the head (head diameter 40–70 % of frame height).
- Frequent **blurred foreground silhouette** (a shoulder, a head, a tree, a railing) framing the subject.

## What it is NOT
- Not cel-shaded anime, not 3D, no thick black outlines, no saturated primaries on large areas,
  no pure white backgrounds, no flat unlit compositions.
