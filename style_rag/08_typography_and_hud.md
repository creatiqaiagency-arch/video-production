# 08 — Typography & HUD

## Title card (reference t=33 s)
- Heavy rounded sans, ALL CAPS (we use **Inter Display Black/ExtraBold**; fall back to Montserrat Black).
- Two lines: smaller first line, bigger second line. Colour warm cream `#ffe9c2` with an orange-gold outer glow (`#ff9a3c`, blur 12–20 px).
- Thin rule under the title, then a small spaced-out subtitle ("A STICKMAN WHAT-IF").
- Sits over a blurred establishing shot of the main location.

## Timers / measurements (reference t=29 s, 141 s, 200 s)
- Small clock icon (circle + two hands) + value, e.g. `0.25 s`, in cream with soft glow, top-right or near the action.
- Measurement lines: thin cream lines with end ticks and a label (`11 m`, `160 km/h = 0.4 s`).
- Score counters: `9 / 10`, `VICTORY x28` — big glowing gold.

## Our 5-second HUD
- Countdown `5.0 s → 0.0 s` in the top-right corner during the climax; it **keeps running in the present** while previews reset (auditor edit #3).
- Preview frames get a thin rounded blue border + small label `+5 s` top-left.
- Time stamp `8:17 PM` in cold open as a small cream label.
- "THREE DAYS EARLIER" — spaced caps, cream, centred, gentle glow, 2 s hold.
